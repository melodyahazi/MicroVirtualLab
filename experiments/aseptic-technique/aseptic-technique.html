EXPERIMENT 02

Aseptic Techniques

Learn the principles and practices used to prevent contamination during microbiological laboratory activities.

🧫

Introduction

Aseptic technique refers to the practices used to prevent unwanted microorganisms from contaminating cultures, materials and laboratory work areas.

Proper aseptic practices help maintain the purity of cultures and reduce the risk of contamination during microbiological work.

🧤

Principle

Aseptic technique is based on minimizing opportunities for microorganisms to enter sterile materials and cultures.

Good organization, appropriate handling of materials and minimizing unnecessary exposure are important parts of aseptic practice.

🎯

ASEPTIC PRACTICES

Important Practices

🧹

Clean Work Area

Keep the work area organized and appropriately prepared before beginning laboratory activities.

🧴

Proper Handling

Handle laboratory materials carefully and minimize unnecessary exposure of containers and cultures.

🏷️

Proper Labeling

Clearly identify laboratory containers and samples before proceeding with laboratory activities.

🧤

Personal Protection

Use appropriate laboratory protective practices when performing microbiological activities.
