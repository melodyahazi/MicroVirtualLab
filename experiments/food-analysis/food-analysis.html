Microbial Analysis of Food Samples

This virtual learning activity introduces the concepts involved in microbial analysis of food samples, including dilution, colony counting, and interpretation of microbial counts.

Learning focus: Food sample analysis, decimal dilution, colony counts, countable plates, and colony-forming units (CFU).

Learning Objectives

After completing this activity, you should be able to:

01

Understand Food Sample Analysis

Explain the purpose of analysing microorganisms associated with food samples.

02

Understand Dilution

Recognize the role of decimal dilution in microbial analysis.

03

Interpret Colony Counts

Identify the purpose of selecting a suitable countable plate.

04

Understand CFU

Explain what colony-forming units represent in microbial analysis.

Key Concepts

🍽️

Food Sample

A food sample can be examined to estimate the microorganisms associated with the sample.

🔢

Decimal Dilution

Decimal dilution is used to obtain progressively diluted samples so that microbial numbers can be assessed through colony counts.

🔬

Colony Count

A colony count represents the number of visible colonies observed on a suitable plate.

📊

CFU

CFU, or colony-forming units, is used to express an estimated number of viable microorganisms represented by the colonies counted.
