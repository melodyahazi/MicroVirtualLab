EXPERIMENT 01

Use and Handling of Microscope

Learn the basic principles, care and proper handling of a laboratory microscope before entering the virtual laboratory.

🔬

Introduction

A microscope is a laboratory instrument used to magnify small objects so that they can be seen clearly.

In microbiology, it is mainly used to observe microorganisms, cells, and cell structures that are invisible to the naked eye.

💡

Principle

The microscope works on the principle of magnification and resolution.

Light passes through the specimen and lenses. The objective lens magnifies the image while the eyepiece further enlarges it.

🛡️

BEFORE YOU START

Microscope Care & Handling

01

Carry the microscope using both hands, keeping it upright.

02

Place the microscope safely on the laboratory table.

03

Switch on the light source or adjust the mirror.

04

Place the prepared slide on the stage and secure it with clips.

05

Start with the low-power objective lens.

06

Use the coarse adjustment knob to focus the image.

07

Use the fine adjustment knob to sharpen the image.

08

Adjust the diaphragm to improve brightness and clarity.
