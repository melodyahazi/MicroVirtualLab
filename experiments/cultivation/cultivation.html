EXPERIMENT 03

Cultivation of Bacteria

Learn how bacterial cultures are cultivated using appropriate microbiological techniques and observe simulated bacterial growth.

🎯

Aim

To cultivate bacteria using appropriate microbiological culture techniques and observe the resulting bacterial growth.

🧰

Materials

🧫

Culture plates

🦠

Bacterial sample

🪄

Inoculating loop

🔥

Bunsen burner

🧤

Laboratory gloves

🏷️

Labels

💡

Principle

Bacteria can be cultivated on suitable culture media under appropriate conditions. Proper inoculation and aseptic handling help obtain an observable bacterial culture while reducing unwanted contamination.

📋

Procedure

  1. Prepare and organize the laboratory work area.
  2. Select the appropriate bacterial sample and culture plate.
  3. Prepare the inoculating loop for use.
  4. Transfer the bacterial sample to the culture plate using the appropriate inoculation technique.
  5. Complete the inoculation and close the culture plate appropriately.
  6. Label the culture plate.
  7. Place the culture plate under the appropriate simulated incubation conditions.
  8. Observe the simulated bacterial growth and record the result.
